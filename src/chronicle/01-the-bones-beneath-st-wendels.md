Rain had been falling on Dunmere for three days when we came to it, and the innkeeper, Mother Hessel, did not ask our business until we had eaten. When she did, it was to tell us that the dead in Saint Wendel's churchyard had taken to walking, and that the village would pay in silver to have them lie down again.

Brother Anselm, a travelling pardoner with very clean hands, offered to sell us a blessing for the task. We declined. He looked disappointed for about as long as it took to order another jug.

## The churchyard

We went at dusk. Aldric took the gate, Grimbold took the wall, and Morwen took one look at the open grave nearest the chapel and announced that something had climbed *out* of it rather than been dug up.

She was right. Three of them came at us between the yews, bone grinding on bone. Aldric's sword did good work. Grimbold's axe did louder work.

## The crypt

Beneath the chapel we found the cause: a cracked reliquary, its saint's bones scattered, and a smell no rat could account for. A rat tried anyway. Morwen dealt with it.

The reliquary bears a seal none of us recognised. Brother Anselm, when shown a drawing of it later, recognised it immediately and then claimed not to.
