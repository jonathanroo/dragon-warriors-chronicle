Brian Enorath was born in Ogg, a tiny village in South Emphidor. Ogg is exactly what you'd expect a barbarian village to be: packed with nutters in loincloths, dragging their knuckles and scrapping over rocks. Everyone is an idiot.

But Brian's family was different. His parents were whacked-out, peace-loving wannabe druids. Their idea of a good time was listening to the local durge organ player when he rolled through town. But Brian couldn't stand it! He couldn't understand his parents or his younger brother, Aphex, at all. He longed for the wilderness and the thrill of the wild.

One evening, Brian lost it. His mum had been playing the 74-minute tune "Gentle Flatulence III" on the bagpipes when he suddenly stood up. "I can't stand this any … more!" he shouted, with some effort, and he stormed out of the bivouac and out of the tribe.

He almost instantly regretted it: it was dark in the wild; he was hungry and also, after wandering for miles and miles, he was definitely lost. Still, there was no turning back now. He just had to follow the trail…

After many months Brian has found himself much further north than he'd ever thought possible. It's colder, darker and the land is populated by pale and unusual inhabitants. As he wanders into the Vale of Glissom, he wonders whether this may be the place where he starts to find a sense of purpose… or maybe the place where he can finally get the sound of the durge organ out of his head!
