title: Sir Nockturn
profession: Knight
player: Luke
epithet: Stupid but self-assured. Bears a hedgehog upon his surcoat and seeks a golden sword.
---

A knight of the road with a hedgehog on his surcoat and a quest for a golden sword. He has a great deal of confidence and somewhat less of everything else. He took against the sorcerer sent to help him within moments of meeting him, and the feeling was mutual.

At the inn in Maiden's Vale he was the first to the door when the sorcerer blew it open, and so was knocked senseless by it.
