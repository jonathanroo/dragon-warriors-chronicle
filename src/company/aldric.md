Aldric left Thornwood with his father's blessing and his elder brother's horse, which he insists was a gift. He fights like he was trained to, by the book and with a certain courtesy, and is privately terrified of the dead.

## Deeds

- Held the churchyard gate against two of the risen dead at Dunmere.
