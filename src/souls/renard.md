The curious traveller on the road asked us to look after his friend Renard, if we ever met him. We assumed Renard was a man.

Then, outside Maiden's Vale, we found a fox caught in a trap with Biff's knife at its throat. We set it free, and Pedium offered it a bobbit. Biff called it "a filthy Renard".

The company has its suspicions.
