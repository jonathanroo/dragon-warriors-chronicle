kind: miscreant
role: Guide from Maiden's Vale

opinion: Chosen for his size. Regretted for his knife.
summary: The fat one. We picked him as our guide. He tried to cut a fox's throat.
---

One of three men brought forth to guide us out of Maiden's Vale. We picked the fat one. Not long into the journey he had us wait while he went to check on a noise, and when we followed we found him over a fox caught in a trap, knife in hand.

Brian pulled him off. When Pedium offered the fox a bobbit, Biff called it a waste of good bobbit on "a filthy Renard", decided it was going to be like that, and stomped off.
