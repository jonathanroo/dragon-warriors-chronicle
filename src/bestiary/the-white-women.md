---
title: The White Women
kind: Apparition
habitat: The fog of Maiden's Vale, by night
summary: Beautiful women in white, until they come closer.
---

They come gliding out of the fog in the moonlight, two figures in white. At a distance they are beautiful women. Closer, their faces are twisted with misery and pain. Closer still, the flesh falls from their faces until they are skeletons in rags.

They ask, in a terrible rasping whisper: *"Where… hope?"*

When Rudious Greyrat held out a bobbit to them, they screamed and were sucked away into the night. The bobbit was dead soon after.
