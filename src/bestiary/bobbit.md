Bobbits are everywhere in Maiden's Vale. They whimper and cry at night. The villagers love them, keep them as pets and eat them, which they have done for years: bobbit stew and bobbit ale have kept the village alive. They do not taste great.

Dumplina the barmaid keeps them as pets. One of hers did not survive the night of the white women.

They are also served on toast. The company set out from the village carrying several live ones, and Pedium tried feeding one to a fox.
